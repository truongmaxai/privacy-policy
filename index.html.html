Privacy Policy – Minh Hung Post

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Minh Hung Post ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you use the App, including when you log in with Facebook. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

When you log in with Facebook and grant permission, we may receive only the information you approve, such as:

  • Your public profile (name, profile picture) and Facebook user ID
  • Your email address, if you choose to share it
  • A list of Facebook Pages you manage and the access tokens needed to publish content to those Pages on your instruction
  • Content you create or schedule through the App (text, images, links)

We do not collect your Facebook password and we do not access private messages unless you explicitly grant that permission for a feature you use.

2. How we use your information

  • To log you in and identify your account
  • To publish or schedule posts to the Pages you select, only when you instruct the App to do so
  • To show you the status of your posts
  • To provide support and respond to your requests

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not use Facebook data for advertising or profiling.

3. Sharing of information

We share data only with Meta Platforms, Inc. through its official APIs in order to perform the actions you request, or when required by law. We do not share your data with any other third party.

4. Data storage and security

Your data is stored on secured servers with access limited to authorized personnel. Access tokens are encrypted and are used only for the purposes described above. We keep your data only as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service.

5. Data deletion

You can request deletion of all data the App holds about you at any time:

  • Option 1: Go to Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, find "Minh Hung Post" and click Remove. This revokes the App's access.
  • Option 2: Email nguoinamcham90@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and your Facebook name or user ID.

We will delete your data within 30 days of receiving your request and confirm by email.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not intended for anyone under 18 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at nguoinamcham90@gmail.com.
