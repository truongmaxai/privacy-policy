Data Deletion Instructions – Minh Hung Post

You can request deletion of all data that Minh Hung Post holds about you at any time.

Option 1: Remove the app from Facebook

  1. Go to your Facebook account: Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Open Apps and websites.
  3. Find Minh Hung Post and click Remove.

This revokes the app's access to your Facebook account.

Option 2: Email us

Send an email to nguoinamcham90@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and include your Facebook name or user ID.

We will delete all of your data within 30 days of receiving your request and confirm by email.
